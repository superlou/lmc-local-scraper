# The Pediatric Workshop

EXO Workshops presents The Pediatric Workshop, life-saving first aid for kids and infants. Perfect for parents, caregivers, expectant mothers, nannies, this 90 min workshop is designed to promote confidence and teamwork to ensure you react quickly and effectively in emergencies.

Participants will be performing the following skills: CPR, Choking Relief, Seizure Response, EPI-PEN, and more!
